Avocado and Pistacho Smoothie

Description :

Made with avocado, banana, and spinach, this Avocado Green Smoothie is the ultimate energizer for your mornings with a delightfully addictive taste. Topped with chopped pistachios, this smoothie combination is truly the winner of all.

Ingredients :

  • 1 medium ripe avocado
  • 1 medium ripe banana, frozen
  • 1 cup spinach, frozen. This can be substituted with other greens of choice if needed (kale, rainbow chard)
  • 1 1/2 cup milk, to be substituted with almond or soy milk for a dairy-free option
  • 1 tbsp of yogurt, can be skipped for a dairy-free option
  • Optional add-ins:
    • 1 scoop plain or vanilla protein powder
    • 1 tbsp seed of choice (hemp, flax, sesame, chia, sunflower, etc.)
    • chopped pistachios for serving

Steps :

  1. In a high-speed blender, combine frozen banana, avocado, spinach, milk, and yogurt. At this time, add any optional add-ins, such as protein powder or seeds, or an ingredient of your choice.
  2. Blend until creamy and smooth, ensuring there are no chunks left behind. Adjust sweetness by adding more frozen banana if needed. Serve immediately or store in the refrigerator for upto 24 hours.
